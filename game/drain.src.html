    Where the data and drawings come from

    Drug names, and which drug blocks, destroys or multiplies each enzyme, come from OpenADMET's CYP and PXR data. Timing and strengths are tuned for play. Keyboard: Space doses the first beaker, 1 to 3 the others, and R, I or B uses the tokens in the corner. The enzymes are crystal structures of CYP2D6 (PDB 2F9Q) and CYP3A4 (PDB 1TQN), and the drugs are their 3D shapes, drawn with Illustrate by David S. Goodsell.
